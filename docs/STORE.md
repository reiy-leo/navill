# 应用商店文案

## 简介（manifest description，≤132 字符，见 public/_locales）
- 中文：把浏览器书签整理成工作区和抽屉，支持场景、分身，可同步到 Google Drive 或 WebDAV，还能把抽屉和工作区分享给他人安装。
- English: Reorganize bookmarks into workspaces and drawers, with modes and aliases, sync via Google Drive or WebDAV, and share with others.

## 详细说明（与扩展内介绍 src/ui/Intro.tsx 保持一致）

### 中文
导入浏览器书签后，你可以按用途把它们重新整理成多个工作区，例如影视、AI、工作、软件安装、数据分析、色彩管理。工作区内可以再用抽屉细分，也可以不分；抽屉支持多种布局。

工作区和抽屉可以按使用场景分组，例如演讲模式、娱乐模式。切换场景后，只显示当下需要的内容。

书签和抽屉可以“分身”到其他抽屉或工作区，同一份内容出现在多处。遇到难以只归到一处的书签，不必再纠结放在哪里。

书签和设置可以同步到 Google Drive 或 WebDAV，在多个浏览器之间保持一致。

你还可以把整理好的抽屉或工作区分享出去，别人一键就能安装使用。

### English
Import your browser bookmarks and reorganize them by purpose into workspaces, such as Movies, AI, Work, Software, Data Analysis, and Color Management. Split a workspace further into drawers, or leave it as is; drawers come in several layouts.

Group workspaces and drawers into modes, such as a Presentation mode or an Entertainment mode. Switch modes to see only what you need right now.

Alias bookmarks and drawers into other drawers or workspaces, so the same item can live in several places. No more agonizing over where a hard-to-classify bookmark belongs.

Sync bookmarks and settings to Google Drive or WebDAV to keep them consistent across browsers.

Share a drawer or workspace you have organized, and others can install it in one click.
