# Privacy and Permissions

Navill collects no data. Your bookmarks stay in the browser and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive or WebDAV server. Full policy: [https://navill.rei8t.dev/privacy](https://navill.rei8t.dev/privacy).

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| bookmarks | Read browser bookmarks for the import |
| storage, unlimitedStorage | Keep your data in the browser |
| favicon (not on Firefox) | Show site icons |
| identity | Sign in to Google for sync and sharing |
| alarms | Run scheduled sync |
| googleapis.com | Google Drive sync |
| icon.horse, google.com/s2, gstatic.com | Fetch sharper site icons |
| navill.rei8t.dev | Sharing and the market |
| history (optional) | Suggest pages while you add a bookmark |
| all sites (optional) | Fetch icons from the sites themselves; reach your WebDAV server |
