# Navill

A new tab page for people with too many bookmarks. Import your browser bookmarks once, then reorganize them as **Workspace → Drawer → Bookmark**. Navill keeps its own data and never writes back to the browser's bookmarks.

Start with [[Install]] and [[Getting Started|Getting-Started]], then browse the sidebar.
