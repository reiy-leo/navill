# Sharing and Market

## Share

Choose **Share** in a drawer's ⋯ menu or a workspace's right-click menu. Sign in with Google (it only reads your email to confirm who you are), add an author name and a short description, and share.

- A share is reviewed before it goes public. Its card shows the status: in review, public or rejected.
- 18+ workspaces and drawers can't be shared; 18+ content inside a share is left out.
- A share can't be edited. To change it, stop sharing in **Manage shares** and share again.
- The sharing sign-in is separate from the Google Drive sync sign-in.

## Market

Approved shares appear on the [market site](https://navill.rei8t.dev), in the market view inside Navill, and in **Latest in market** on Home. Use **Install to…** on any drawer to copy it into one of your workspaces, related links included.
