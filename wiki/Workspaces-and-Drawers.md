# Workspaces and Drawers

## Workspaces

A workspace is a top-level group, such as Work, AI or Movies. You can have up to 30, Inbox included.

- Right-click a workspace to edit its name, icon (emoji or a line icon), color and description, mark it 18+, share it, or delete it.
- Drag workspaces to reorder them.
- The workspace bar can sit on the left, right, top or bottom (Settings → Look), and show icons, names, or both.

## Drawers

Drawers split a workspace into smaller groups. You don't have to use them: bookmarks can sit directly in a workspace, above its drawers.

Each drawer's title bar has:

- **View**: Icons, Rows, Tiles or Bento. A drawer follows its workspace's view unless you set its own.
- **Sort**: by name or by open count, ascending or descending. Dragging switches back to manual order.
- **⋯ menu**: create an alias group, share, archive, dissolve (keeps the bookmarks at the workspace top level), delete, shuffle.
- Double-click the title to rename. Drag the bottom edge to change its height.

## Arrange

The workspace title bar's arrange button lays drawers out as **Strip** (one drawer per row, scrolls sideways), **Masonry** (default), **Bento** (grid) or **Board** (one column per drawer). It's remembered per workspace on each device.

## Home

**Home** is the first item in the workspace bar. It shows your **Favorites**, your **Most used** bookmarks, and the **Latest in market** shares.

## Archive

Archive a bookmark or a drawer to hide it everywhere without deleting it. Open the archive from the ⋯ menu to restore or delete items. Search leaves archived items out unless you tick **Include archived**.
